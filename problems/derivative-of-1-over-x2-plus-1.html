Derivative of 1/(x^2+1)

dxd​[x2+11​]

Answer

f′(x)=(x2+1)2−2⋅x​

✓ Checked: agrees with an independent numerical differentiation at 5 sample points.

Step by step

  1. Quotient rule

    d/dx[u/w] = (u′·w − u·w′)/w² with u = 1, w = x ^ 2 + 1.

    dxd​[x2+11​]=−(x2+1)22⋅x​
  2. Sum rule

    Differentiate each term separately.

    dxd​[x2+1]=2⋅x
  3. Power rule

    d/dx[xⁿ] = n·xⁿ⁻¹.

    dxd​[x2]=2⋅x
  4. Simplify

    Result: -2 * x / (x ^ 2 + 1) ^ 2

    (x2+1)2−2⋅x​
