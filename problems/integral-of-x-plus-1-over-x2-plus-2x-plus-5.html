Integral of (x+1)/(x²+2x+5)

Step by step

  1. Factor the denominator

    Roots of the denominator: x = -1 ± 2i.

  2. Partial fraction decomposition

    (x + 1) / (x^2 + 2 x + 5) = (1*x + 1)/(x^2 - -2*x + 5)

  3. Integrate term by term

    Simple poles give logarithms, irreducible quadratics give logarithms and arctangents, and repeated factors give powers.

    2ln((x+1)2+4)​
  4. Add the constant of integration

    Every antiderivative differs by a constant, so we add + C.

    2ln(​(x+1)2+4​)​+C
  5. Check by differentiating

    d/dx [ log(abs((x + 1)^2 + 4)) / 2 ] = (x + 1) / ((x + 1)^2 + 4), which matches the integrand.

    dxd​​2ln(​(x+1)2+4​)​​=(x+1)2+4x+1​

About this kind of problem

An antiderivative is a function whose derivative is the one you were given. The working names the technique (table, substitution, by parts or partial fractions) and every answer is differentiated back to confirm it.

For this problem the working uses: Factor the denominator, Partial fraction decomposition, Integrate term by term, Add the constant of integration, Check by differentiating.
