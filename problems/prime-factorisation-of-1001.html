Prime factorisation of 1001

1001=?

Answer

1001=7⋅11⋅13

✓ Checked: the factors multiply back to 1001.

  • Number of divisors τ(n): 8
  • Sum of divisors σ(n): 1344
  • Euler's totient φ(n): 720
  • Prime? no
  • Divisors: 1, 7, 11, 13, 77, 91, 143, 1001

Open this in the calculatorNumber theory calculator

Step by step

  1. Divide out the primes one at a time

    1001 ÷ 7 = 143; 143 ÷ 11 = 13; 13 ÷ 13 = 1. The quotient reaches 1, so nothing is left.

  2. Collect equal factors

    1001 = 7 · 11 · 13

    1001=7⋅11⋅13
  3. Check by multiplying back

    7, 11, 13 → product = 1001

  4. What the factors tell you

    The number of divisors is the product of (exponent + 1) = 8; the sum of divisors is the product of (p^(e+1) − 1)/(p − 1) = 1344.

About this kind of problem

Large integers are factored with trial division and Pollard’s rho, and primality is tested with Miller–Rabin. Every factorisation is multiplied back to confirm it.

For this problem the working uses: Divide out the primes one at a time, Collect equal factors, Check by multiplying back, What the factors tell you.
