Step by step

  1. Eliminate below each pivot, recording the multipliers

    swap R1 ↔ R2 (the larger pivot 6 is safer); R2 → R2 − (2/3)·R1. The multipliers (the numbers in brackets) go into L below the diagonal, and the swaps are recorded in P so that PA = LU; the rows that are left form U = [[6, 3], [0, 1]].

  2. Eliminate below each pivot

    Record every multiplier ℓᵢⱼ in L and keep the reduced rows in U; swap rows (tracked in P) when a larger pivot is available.

About this kind of problem

Every matrix operation is worked row by row, and the result is verified: a determinant against a second expansion, an inverse by multiplying it with the original, eigenvalues by substituting back.
