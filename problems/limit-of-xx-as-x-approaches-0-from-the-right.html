Step by step

  1. Try direct substitution

    f(0) = 1 – the function is defined here.

  2. Approach from both sides

    Evaluate f at x = 0 ± 10⁻², 10⁻³, … 10⁻⁹.

  3. Compare

    Left → does not exist, right → 1.

About this kind of problem

A limit describes what a function approaches near a point or at infinity. Indeterminate forms such as 0/0 are worked through with L'Hôpital's rule, and the left and right approaches are compared so jumps and infinite limits are not missed.

For this problem the working uses: Try direct substitution, Approach from both sides, Compare.
