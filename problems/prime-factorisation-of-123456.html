Prime factorisation of 123456

123456=?

Answer

123456=26⋅3⋅643

✓ Checked: the factors multiply back to 123456.

  • Number of divisors τ(n): 28
  • Sum of divisors σ(n): 327152
  • Euler's totient φ(n): 41088
  • Prime? no
  • Divisors: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 192, 643, 1286, 1929, 2572, 3858, 5144, 7716, 10288, 15432, 20576, 30864, 41152, 61728, 123456

Step by step

  1. Divide out the primes one at a time

    123456 ÷ 2 = 61728; 61728 ÷ 2 = 30864; 30864 ÷ 2 = 15432; 15432 ÷ 2 = 7716; 7716 ÷ 2 = 3858; 3858 ÷ 2 = 1929; 1929 ÷ 3 = 643; 643 ÷ 643 = 1. The quotient reaches 1, so nothing is left.

  2. Collect equal factors

    123456 = 2⁶ · 3 · 643

    123456=26⋅3⋅643
  3. Check by multiplying back

    2⁶ = 64, 3, 643 → product = 123456

  4. What the factors tell you

    The number of divisors is the product of (exponent + 1) = 28; the sum of divisors is the product of (p^(e+1) − 1)/(p − 1) = 327152.

About this kind of problem

Large integers are factored with trial division and Pollard’s rho, and primality is tested with Miller–Rabin. Every factorisation is multiplied back to confirm it.

For this problem the working uses: Divide out the primes one at a time, Collect equal factors, Check by multiplying back, What the factors tell you.
