Derivative of e^(2x)

dxd​[e2 x]

Answer

f′(x)=2⋅e2 x

✓ Checked: agrees with an independent numerical differentiation at 5 sample points.

Open this in the calculatorDerivative calculator with steps

Step by step

  1. Exponential rule

    d/dx[aᵘ] = aᵘ·ln(a)·u′ with a = e, u = 2 x.

    dxd​[e2 x]=2⋅e2 x
  2. Constant multiple rule

    Constants factor out: d/dx[c·f] = c·f′.

    dxd​[2 x]=2
  3. Simplify

    Result: 2 * e ^ (2 x)

    2⋅e2 x

About this kind of problem

The derivative measures how fast a function changes. The working below names every rule used (sum, product, quotient, chain and the standard derivatives) and the answer is compared with a numerical derivative before it is shown.
