Integral of x eˣ from 0 to 1

Step by step

  1. Worked solution (Integration by parts)

    The steps below show one valid technique; every antiderivative agrees up to the constant C.

  2. Integration by parts (round 1)

    Choose u = x and dv = e^x dx (LIATE rule). Then du = 1 dx and v = exp(x).

    u=x,dv=exdx⇒du=1dx,v=exp(x)
  3. Apply ∫u dv = uv − ∫v du

    The remaining integral is ∫ exp(x) dx.

    ∫exp(x)dx
  4. Assemble the result

    F(x) = exp(x) * (x - 1)

    F(x)=exp(x)⋅(x−1)
  5. Antiderivative

    F(x) = -exp(x) + x * exp(x)

    F(x)=−exp(x)+x⋅exp(x)
  6. Apply the Fundamental Theorem

    ∫ f dx = F(1) − F(0)

    F(1)−F(0)
  7. Evaluate

    = 1 ≈ 1

    1≈1

About this kind of problem

A definite integral is the signed area between a curve and the x-axis. It is found from an antiderivative and the fundamental theorem of calculus, or numerically when no antiderivative exists, and the value is checked a second way.

For this problem the working uses: Worked solution (Integration by parts), Integration by parts (round 1), Apply ∫u dv = uv − ∫v du, Assemble the result, Antiderivative, Apply the Fundamental Theorem.
