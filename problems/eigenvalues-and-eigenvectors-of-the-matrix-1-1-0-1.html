Eigenvalues and eigenvectors of the matrix [[1, 1], [0, 1]]

[10​11​]

Answer

λ=1

✓ Checked: max |A·v − λ·v| = 0.00e+0 (eigenvectors normalised to unit length)

  • A = [ [1, 1], [0, 1] ]
  • λ = 1: multiplicity 2 (only 1 independent eigenvector – defective); (1, 0)

Open this in the calculatorMatrix calculator

Step by step

  1. Characteristic equation

    det(λI − A) = 0 is solved for λ (all complex roots, with multiplicity).

    det(λI−A)=0
  2. Eigenvectors

    For each real λ the null space of (A − λI) is computed by row reduction.

    (A−λI)v=0

About this kind of problem

Every matrix operation is worked row by row, and the result is verified: a determinant against a second expansion, an inverse by multiplying it with the original, eigenvalues by substituting back.

For this problem the working uses: Characteristic equation, Eigenvectors.
