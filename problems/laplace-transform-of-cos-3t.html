Laplace transform of cos(3t)

L{cos(3 t)}

Answer

F(s)=s2+9s​

✓ Checked: ∫₀^∞ f(t)e^(−st) dt = 0.14297729 at s = 1.7 matches F(s).

  • Region of convergence: Re(s) > 0
  • Expanded denominator: s^2 + 9

Step by step

  1. Rewrite f(t) as a sum of terms tⁿ·e^{zt}

    Products of exponentials and trigonometric functions are expanded with Euler’s formula so every term has the form c·tⁿ·e^{zt}.

    f(t)=k∑​ck​tnk​ezk​t
  2. Transform 1/2·t·e^(0−3it)

    ℒ{tⁿe^{zt}} = n!/(s − z)ⁿ⁺¹ with n = 0, z = 0−3i

    L{t0ezt}=(s−z)11​
  3. Transform 1/2·t·e^(0+3it)

    ℒ{tⁿe^{zt}} = n!/(s − z)ⁿ⁺¹ with n = 0, z = 0+3i

    L{t0ezt}=(s−z)11​
  4. Combine over a common denominator

    F(s) = (s) / ((s^2 + 9))

    F(s)=(s2+9)s​

About this kind of problem

The Laplace transform turns a function of t into a function of s, which turns differential equations into algebra. The answer is built term by term from the standard table and checked by integrating e^(−st)·f(t) numerically.

For this problem the working uses: Rewrite f(t) as a sum of terms tⁿ·e^{zt}, Transform 1/2·t·e^(0−3it), Transform 1/2·t·e^(0+3it), Combine over a common denominator.
