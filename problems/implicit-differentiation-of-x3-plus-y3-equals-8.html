Implicit differentiation of x^3 + y^3 = 8

find dxdy​ if x3+y3=8

Answer

dxdy​=y2−x2​

✓ Checked: solving the equation numerically for y just either side of 3 points on the curve gives the same slope as the formula (largest relative gap 3.1e-9).

Open this in the calculatorImplicit differentiation calculator

Step by step

  1. Put everything on one side

    F(x, y) = x ^ 3 + y ^ 3 - 8 = 0

    F(x,y)=x3+y3−8=0
  2. Differentiate both sides with respect to x

    Treat y as a function of x; the chain rule gives F_x + F_y · y′ = 0, with F_x = 3 * x ^ 2 and F_y = 3 * y ^ 2.

    Fx​+Fy​y′=0
  3. Solve for y′

    y′ = −F_x / F_y = -x ^ 2 / y ^ 2

    y′=−Fy​Fx​​=y2−x2​

About this kind of problem

When y is defined by an equation instead of a formula, differentiate both sides with respect to x, apply the chain rule to every term containing y, and solve for dy/dx.

For this problem the working uses: Put everything on one side, Differentiate both sides with respect to x, Solve for y′.
