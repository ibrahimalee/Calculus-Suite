Is 561 a prime number?

is 561 prime?

Answer

561 is composite
  • Factorisation: 3 · 11 · 17

Open this in the calculatorNumber theory calculator

Step by step

  1. A small factor settles it

    561 ÷ 3 = 187 with remainder 0, so 3 divides 561 and 561 is composite.

About this kind of problem

Large integers are factored with trial division and Pollard’s rho, and primality is tested with Miller–Rabin. Every factorisation is multiplied back to confirm it.

For this problem the working uses: A small factor settles it.
