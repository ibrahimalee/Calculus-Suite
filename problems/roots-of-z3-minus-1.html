Roots of z³-1

z3−1=0

Answer

z = 1, -1/2 + 0.8660254i, -1/2 − 0.8660254i

✓ Checked: max |p(root)| = 6.80e-16

  • Degree: 3
  • Moduli: 1, 1, 1

Open this in the calculatorComplex number calculator

Step by step

  1. Fundamental theorem of algebra

    A degree-3 polynomial has exactly that many complex roots (counted with multiplicity).

  2. Simultaneous root finding

    All roots are refined together (Aberth iteration) and checked by substitution.

About this kind of problem

The roots of a polynomial are found numerically and shown exactly where recognised, and each root is substituted back into the polynomial.

For this problem the working uses: Fundamental theorem of algebra, Simultaneous root finding.
