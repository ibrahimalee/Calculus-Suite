Inverse Laplace transform of (s+1)/(s^2+2s+2)

L−1{s2+2 s+2s+1​}

Answer

f(t)=exp(−t)⋅cos(t);(t≥0)

✓ Checked: ∫₀^∞ f(t)e^(−st) dt reproduces F(s) at s = 1.9.

  • F(s) = (s + 1) / (s ^ 2 + 2 s + 2)

Open this in the calculatorLaplace transform calculator

Step by step

  1. Check properness

    Numerator degree 1 < denominator degree 2.

  2. Find the poles

    -1 ± 1i

  3. Partial fractions

    Write F(s) = Σ cⱼ/(s − p)ʲ and solve for the cⱼ.

  4. Invert term by term

    ℒ⁻¹{1/(s − p)ʲ} = t^{j−1}e^{pt}/(j−1)!; conjugate pairs combine into cos and sin.

    f(t)=exp(−t)⋅cos(t)

About this kind of problem

The inverse Laplace transform recovers f(t) from F(s). The fraction is split into partial fractions (including repeated and complex poles) and each piece is read off the table; the result is transformed back to confirm it.

For this problem the working uses: Check properness, Find the poles, Partial fractions, Invert term by term.
