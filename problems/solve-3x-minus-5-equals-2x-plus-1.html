Solve 3x-5 = 2x+1

3 x−5=2 x+1

Answer

x=6
  • x ≈ 6 residual 0.0e+0

Open this in the calculatorEquation and inequality solver

Step by step

  1. Bring everything to one side

    3 x - 5 − (2 x + 1) = 0

    3 x−5−(2 x+1)=0
  2. Collect like terms

    1·x + (-6) = 0

    1x+(−6)=0
  3. Isolate x

    1·x = 6

    1x=6
  4. Divide by 1

    x = 6

    x=6
  5. Check

    f(6) = 0.0e+0

About this kind of problem

Polynomial equations are factored and solved exactly (with complex roots where needed); other equations are solved numerically on a range. Every root is substituted back into the original equation, and inequalities are tested interval by interval.

For this problem the working uses: Bring everything to one side, Collect like terms, Isolate x, Divide by 1, Check.
