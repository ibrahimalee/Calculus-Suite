Derivative of (x^2+1)/(x^2-1)

Step by step

  1. Quotient rule

    d/dx[u/w] = (u′·w − u·w′)/w² with u = x^2 + 1, w = x^2 - 1.

    dxd​[x2−1x2+1​]=2⋅(x2−11​−(x2−11​)2⋅(x2+1))⋅x
  2. Sum rule

    Differentiate each term separately.

    dxd​[x2+1]=2⋅x
  3. Power rule

    d/dx[xⁿ] = n·xⁿ⁻¹.

    dxd​[x2]=2⋅x
  4. Difference rule

    Differentiate each term separately.

    dxd​[x2−1]=2⋅x
  5. Power rule

    d/dx[xⁿ] = n·xⁿ⁻¹.

    dxd​[x2]=2⋅x
  6. Simplify

    Result: 2 * (1 / (x^2 - 1) - (1 / (x^2 - 1))^2 * (x^2 + 1)) * x

    2⋅(x2−11​−(x2−11​)2⋅(x2+1))⋅x

About this kind of problem
