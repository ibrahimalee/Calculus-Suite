Partial fractions of (x+1)/(x^2-4)

Step by step

  1. Long division first

    The numerator has lower degree than the denominator, so the fraction is already proper.

  2. Factor the denominator

    Each distinct real root r gives terms A/(x−r), A/(x−r)², … up to its multiplicity; each irreducible quadratic gives (Ax+B)/(quadratic)ᵏ.

  3. Solve for the constants

    Match both sides at enough values of x, which gives one linear equation per unknown.

About this kind of problem

Rational functions are split into partial fractions, and polynomials are factored exactly. The pieces are added back together and compared with the original at many points.

For this problem the working uses: Long division first, Factor the denominator, Solve for the constants.
