About this kind of problem

A Taylor series rewrites a function as a polynomial built from its derivatives at a point. Each coefficient is f⁽ⁿ⁾(a)/n!, and the plot shows how the polynomial hugs the function near the centre.

For this problem the working uses: k = 0, k = 1, k = 2, k = 3, k = 4, k = 5.
