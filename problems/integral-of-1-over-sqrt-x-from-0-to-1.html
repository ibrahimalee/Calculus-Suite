About this kind of problem

A definite integral is the signed area between a curve and the x-axis. It is found from an antiderivative and the fundamental theorem of calculus, or numerically when no antiderivative exists, and the value is checked a second way.

For this problem the working uses: Worked solution (U-substitution), u-substitution, Rewrite in terms of u, Integrate, Substitute back, Assemble the result.
