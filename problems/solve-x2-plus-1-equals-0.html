Solve x^2+1 = 0

x2+1=0

Answer

Solutions: no real solutions
Complex: x = 0 ± 1i
  • Complex: x = 0 ± 1i

Open this in the calculatorEquation and inequality solver

Step by step

  1. Bring everything to one side

    x ^ 2 + 1 − (0) = 0

    x2+1−(0)=0
  2. Standard form

    1·x² + 0·x + 1 = 0

    1x2+(0)x+(1)=0
  3. Quadratic formula

    x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) / 2a

    x=2a−b±b2−4ac​​
  4. Discriminant

    Δ = (0)² − 4·(1)·(1) = -4 → two complex roots

    Δ=−4
  5. Check

    No real root to substitute back.

About this kind of problem

Polynomial equations are factored and solved exactly (with complex roots where needed); other equations are solved numerically on a range. Every root is substituted back into the original equation, and inequalities are tested interval by interval.

For this problem the working uses: Bring everything to one side, Standard form, Quadratic formula, Discriminant, Check.
