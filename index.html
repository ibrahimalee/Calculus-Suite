Good to know

  • Symbolic results are verified. Antiderivatives are re-checked by differentiation at sample points before they are shown.
  • Limits, roots and extrema are numerical – excellent for exploration, but not formal proofs.
  • Not every integral has an elementary closed form (e.g. e^(x²)). Use limits to get a numerical value.
  • Use Share on a result to copy a link that reproduces the whole calculation.
  • Long computations run in a background thread and are stopped automatically after 25 s.
