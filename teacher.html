Classroom · no accounts · no server

Set a worksheet. Share one link. Grade the codes.

Students open your link and see only the questions, with no calculator unless you allow one. When they finish they get a short code to send back. Paste the codes into the gradebook here and everything is marked on your device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

This is meant for homework, practice and low-stakes quizzes. It can’t stop a student from using another tool, and a code can’t prove who wrote it. Your answer key stays in this browser (and in any file you export), never in the student link, unless you switch on practice mode.

Worksheets are saved in this browser. Export a file to keep a backup or move to another computer; it contains your answer key, so keep it private.
