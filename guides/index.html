Guides

Short explanations of the main ideas, each with worked examples that were computed and checked by the calculator.

  • How to find a derivativeThe derivative rules in the order you need them: power, sum, product, quotient and chain rule, each with a worked example you can open and change.
  • The chain rule, explainedWhat the chain rule says, how to spot a composite function, and a method that works every time, with computed examples.
  • Implicit differentiation, explainedHow to find dy/dx when y is not isolated, with the circle and the folium of Descartes worked out and checked.
  • Integration by parts, explainedThe integration by parts formula, how to choose u and dv, and worked examples including x·eˣ and x·ln x.
  • u-substitution, explainedRecognise when substitution works, choose u, change the differential and the limits, with computed examples.
  • Partial fractions, explainedHow to split a rational function into simpler fractions for integration, covering distinct, repeated and irreducible factors.
  • What is a limit?The idea of a limit, how to compute common ones by factoring and with L’Hôpital’s rule, and when a limit does not exist.
  • Taylor series, explainedHow a Taylor series builds a polynomial from derivatives, the standard series to know, and how to see how good the approximation is.
  • How to find critical points and extremaUse the first and second derivative to find local maxima, minima and inflection points, with a worked example you can open.
  • Riemann sums and the definite integralHow adding thin rectangles leads to the definite integral, how left, right, midpoint and trapezoid sums compare, and the fundamental theorem.
  • Laplace transform basicsWhat the Laplace transform does, the standard table, how to invert it with partial fractions, and a worked example.
  • Eigenvalues and eigenvectors, explainedWhat eigenvalues and eigenvectors mean, how to compute them for a 2×2 matrix, and how to check the answer.
