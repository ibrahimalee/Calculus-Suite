What is a limit?

A limit asks what value a function is heading towards, not what it is at the point itself. It is the idea that calculus is built on.

The idea

The limit of f(x) as x approaches a is L if f(x) can be made as close to L as you like by taking x close enough to a, without being equal to a.

x→alim​f(x)=L

Try substitution first

If f is continuous at a, the limit is just f(a). The interesting cases are 0/0 and ∞/∞.

Factor and cancel

For (x² − 4)/(x − 2) at 2, substitution gives 0/0. Factor the top as (x − 2)(x + 2), cancel, and the limit is 4.

When a limit does not exist

If the left and right limits differ, the two-sided limit does not exist. The function |x|/x at 0 jumps from −1 to 1.

Questions

Is the limit the same as the value?

Only when the function is continuous there. A function can have a limit at a point where it is not even defined.
