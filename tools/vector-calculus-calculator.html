How it works

Curves and surfaces are given parametrically. For conservative fields the tool builds the potential function and evaluates the work as f(B) − f(A).

How the answer is checked

Green's and divergence theorems are verified by computing both sides; line integrals by numerical integration.

Questions

How do I know if a field is conservative?

Choose the potential operation: it tests the cross-partials and, if they agree, builds the potential.
