How it works

Sequences built from powers, polynomials and sines or cosines are transformed exactly, with poles and the region of convergence. Inverse transforms use partial fractions in z, including repeated and complex poles and impulses.

How the answer is checked

Forward transforms are checked by summing the series directly; inverse transforms against long division; difference equations against the recursion.

Typing your input

  • Use n as the index, for example 0.5^n or n*2^n.

Questions

What is the region of convergence?

The set of z for which the series converges, |z| > r for a right-sided sequence. It is listed with every transform.
