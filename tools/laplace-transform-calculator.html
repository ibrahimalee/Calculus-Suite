Laplace transform calculator

Laplace and inverse Laplace transforms, plus Fourier and Z-transforms, with the working shown. Step functions, delta impulses and piecewise functions are supported, and initial-value problems can be solved with the Laplace method.

Open the transforms toolFree, no account. Runs in your browser and works offline.

How it works

Forward transforms use linearity and the standard table, with shifts for e^(at) and u(t − a). Inverse transforms split F(s) into partial fractions, including repeated and complex poles, and read each term off the table.

The same tool does Fourier transforms with standard-pair recognition and Z-transforms for sequences.

How the answer is checked

Forward transforms are checked by integrating e^(−st) f(t) numerically; inverse transforms by transforming the answer back.

Typing your input

  • Write u(t-a) for a step and delta(t-a) for an impulse.
  • For piecewise functions write: expression for condition ; …

Questions

Does it show the region of convergence?

Yes, as Re(s) > σ for the transform of f(t).

Can it solve an ODE with initial conditions?

Yes. Choose the ODE operation, type the equation and give y(0) and y′(0).
