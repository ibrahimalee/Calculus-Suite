How it works

Closed forms are found for linear and separable first-order equations and for constant-coefficient second-order equations, with the integrating factor or characteristic equation shown. Other equations are solved with an adaptive Runge–Kutta method.

Systems get phase portraits, equilibria are classified from the Jacobian, and power-series solutions are available about a point. The heat and wave equations are solved too.

How the answer is checked

A closed-form solution is substituted back into the equation, and compared with a numerical solution from the initial condition.

Typing your input

  • Type the right-hand side of y′ = f(x, y), for example x + y.
  • For second-order equations use yp for y′.
  • Separate the equations of a system with ;

Questions

Does it give a formula or numbers?

A formula when one exists for the equation type, together with a numerical solution and its plot. Otherwise the numerical solution, clearly labelled.

Can it solve systems of ODEs?

Yes, including 3-D systems such as the Lorenz equations, with phase portraits and equilibrium classification for 2-D systems.
