Triangle solver and geometry

Solve any triangle from three of its parts, including the ambiguous SSA case with zero, one or two solutions, and analyse conic sections with their foci and axes.

How it works

Triangles are solved with the laws of sines and cosines; conics are classified and put in standard form, with rotation removed where needed.

How the answer is checked

Solved triangles are checked against both laws and the angle sum; conic foci against the focus–directrix property.

Typing your input

  • Give known sides as a, b, c and angles as A, B, C.

Questions

What is the ambiguous case?

With two sides and an angle not between them (SSA) there can be zero, one or two triangles. The tool lists every one.
