How it works

Improper fractions are first divided; the denominator is factored, and the numerators for linear, repeated and irreducible quadratic factors are found exactly.

How the answer is checked

The partial fractions are added back together and compared with the original at 40 points.

Typing your input

  • Type the rational function as (numerator)/(denominator).

Questions

What happens with a repeated factor?

Each power of the factor gets its own term, for example A/(x+1) + B/(x+1)².
