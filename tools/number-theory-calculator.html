How it works

Factorisation uses trial division and Pollard's rho; primality uses Miller–Rabin. The extended Euclidean algorithm is shown step by step.

How the answer is checked

Factorisations are multiplied back, and modular answers are verified by substitution.

Questions

How large a number can it factor?

Numbers far beyond 64 bits are handled with exact big-integer arithmetic; very hard numbers with two large prime factors can take a while.
