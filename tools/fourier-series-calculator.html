How it works

The coefficients a₀, aₙ and bₙ are computed by integration, using symmetry to skip terms that vanish for even or odd functions. The plot shows the function with successive partial sums.

How the answer is checked

Parseval's identity is checked: the energy of the function equals the sum of the squared coefficients.

Typing your input

  • Fourier coefficients and the partial-sum approximation of a periodic extension.
  • The default interval is [−π, π]; change it with the limits.

Questions

What is the Gibbs phenomenon?

Near a jump, partial sums overshoot by about 9% of the jump however many terms you take. The square-wave example shows it.
