Calculator with exact fractions

A calculator that gives 1/3 + 1/6 = 1/2 rather than 0.5, works with complex numbers and very large integers, and lets you define variables across lines.

How it works

Results are kept exact where possible and shown as fractions, roots or multiples of π; a decimal is shown alongside.

How the answer is checked

Exact results are compared with the floating-point value.

Typing your input

  • A scientific calculator with variables, ans, exact fractions, π-recognition and integer facts.
  • Use ans for the previous answer and name = value to define a variable.

Questions

Can it do complex numbers?

Yes, sqrt(-4) gives 2i, and i can be used in any expression.
