How it works

Each test reports its statistic, degrees of freedom, p-value and decision at your significance level, in plain words. Exact p-values are used for small samples where they exist.

How the answer is checked

p-values are cross-checked by numerical integration of the distribution.

Typing your input

  • Separate values with commas or spaces, and groups with ;

Questions

Which tests are included?

t-tests, chi-square goodness-of-fit and independence, one-way ANOVA, Wilcoxon, Mann–Whitney and Kruskal–Wallis, and proportion intervals.
