How it works

Each method starts from your guess or bracket and records x, f(x) and the change at every step. Newton's method uses the exact derivative; bisection keeps a bracket; the secant method needs two starting points.

How the answer is checked

The final root is substituted into f, and the rate of convergence is shown in the table.

Typing your input

  • Solve f(x) = 0 iteratively and watch every step. Move terms to one side first.
  • Bisection needs an interval [a, b] where f changes sign.

Questions

Why did Newton's method not converge?

It can fail when f′ is zero or small near the iterates, or when there is no real root, as in x² + 1. The example shows what happens.
