Limit calculator

Find the limit of a function as x approaches a number or infinity, from either side. Indeterminate forms are worked through with L'Hôpital's rule, and limits that do not exist are reported as such.

How it works

For 0/0 and ∞/∞ forms the tool differentiates numerator and denominator and shows each round of L'Hôpital's rule. Other limits are evaluated by careful numerical approach from both sides, with the value recognised as a fraction, π or e where possible.

The epsilon–delta task finds, for a chosen ε, the largest δ that works, and draws the ε-band and δ-window.

How the answer is checked

The left and right approaches are computed separately and compared, so a jump or an infinite limit is caught rather than averaged away.

Typing your input

  • Numerical limit analysis from both sides (also at ±∞).
  • Use inf or -inf for limits at infinity.
  • Choose "From the left" or "From the right" for one-sided limits.

Questions

Does it show the steps?

Yes, when L'Hôpital's rule applies each round is shown. Otherwise the result explains how the value was found.

What if the limit does not exist?

The tool reports the two one-sided limits when they differ, for example |x|/x at 0, and says the two-sided limit does not exist.
