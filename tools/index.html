All tools

26 tools in one free app. Each page below explains one of them, with worked examples computed by the tool.

Core calculus

  • Derivative calculator with stepsDifferentiate any function step by step: product, quotient and chain rule, higher derivatives and the tangent line at a point. Every answer is checked numerically.
  • Implicit differentiation calculatorFind dy/dx for an equation in x and y, such as x² + y² = 25, with the steps, the slope and tangent line at a point, second derivatives and a plot of the curve.
  • Integral calculator with stepsIndefinite integrals with the method named: substitution, integration by parts, partial fractions or a table. Each antiderivative is checked by differentiating it.
  • Definite and improper integral calculatorEvaluate definite and improper integrals exactly when possible, numerically otherwise, with divergence detection and the shaded area plotted.
  • Limit calculatorTwo-sided and one-sided limits and limits at infinity, with L'Hôpital steps where they apply and an epsilon–delta explorer. Free, in your browser.
  • Taylor and Maclaurin series calculatorTaylor and Maclaurin polynomials about any point, with the derivative table, the error and a plot. Also Padé approximants.
  • Function analysis: critical points, extrema and asymptotesFind roots, critical points, local maxima and minima, inflection points, asymptotes, and where a function increases or is concave up, with a sketch.

Explore & visualise

  • Graphing calculator with slidersPlot several functions at once, turn any extra letter into an animated slider, and find intersections. Free, offline and in your browser.
  • Riemann sum calculatorLeft, right, midpoint, trapezoid and Simpson sums with the rectangles drawn and a table showing convergence to the integral.
  • Area between curves, volumes and arc lengthArea between curves, volumes by disks, washers and shells, arc length, surface area, average value and curvature, with plots and checks.
  • Root finding: Newton's method, bisection and secantFind roots with Newton's method, bisection or the secant method, with every iteration in a table and the convergence plotted.
  • Fourier series calculatorFourier coefficients, even and odd detection, partial sums you can watch converge, the Gibbs phenomenon and a Parseval check.
  • Triangle solver and geometrySolve triangles from SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS or SSA (the ambiguous case), analyse conic sections, and find circles through three points.

Algebra & sums

  • Calculator with exact fractionsAn online calculator that keeps exact fractions, recognises π and e, handles complex numbers and large integers, and remembers variables across lines.
  • Equation and inequality solverSolve polynomial, rational, trigonometric and transcendental equations and inequalities, with real and complex roots and interval notation.
  • Partial fractions and polynomial algebraPartial fractions with repeated and irreducible quadratic factors, polynomial long division, factoring, gcd and recurrence relations.
  • Linear programming calculatorMaximise or minimise a linear objective with the two-phase simplex method, shadow prices, the feasible region plotted, and infeasible or unbounded cases explained.
  • Series convergence and sumsTest infinite series with the nth-term, ratio, p-series and alternating series tests, find exact sums, radius of convergence and closed forms.
  • Number theory calculatorPrime factorisation, primality tests, gcd with Bézout coefficients, modular inverses and powers, CRT, Diophantine equations, continued fractions and Pell equations.
  • Unit converterConvert speed, temperature, length, volume, pressure, energy and time, with a check that the units are compatible.
  • Statistics calculatorDescriptive statistics, regression, distributions, t-tests, chi-square, ANOVA, nonparametric tests, confidence intervals, Bayes and Markov chains.
  • Truth tables, Boolean algebra and graphsTruth tables, tautology tests, canonical forms and Quine–McCluskey minimisation, plus shortest paths, spanning trees and Euler trails for graphs.

Advanced

  • Differential equation solverSolve first- and second-order ODEs in closed form where possible, numerically otherwise, with slope fields, phase portraits, systems and series solutions.
  • Partial derivatives and multivariable calculusPartial derivatives, gradients, Hessians, Laplacians, critical points of f(x, y) and double integrals, with steps and checks. Free and runs in your browser.
  • Lagrange multipliers calculatorConstrained maxima and minima with Lagrange multipliers, every candidate checked against ∇f = λ∇g, with level curves and the constraint plotted.
  • Vector calculus calculatorLine integrals, work, Green's and divergence theorems, surface integrals and flux, Jacobians, triple integrals and potential functions.
  • Matrix calculatorDeterminants, exact inverses, eigenvalues and eigenvectors, rank, RREF, LU, QR and SVD, linear systems and least squares, with row operations shown.
  • Laplace transform calculatorLaplace transforms, inverse transforms by partial fractions, step and delta functions, piecewise inputs and ODEs solved by Laplace. Every result is checked numerically.
  • Z-transform calculatorZ-transforms of sequences with the region of convergence and poles, inverse Z-transforms by partial fractions, and linear difference equations.
  • Complex number calculatorComplex arithmetic, polar form, all roots of a polynomial, residues and contour integrals, domain colouring and conformal maps.
  • Numerical methods calculatorPolynomial interpolation with divided differences, cubic splines, Chebyshev nodes and the Runge phenomenon, quadrature rules and finite differences.
