How it works

The tool applies the nth-term, ratio, p-series and alternating series tests and explains which one decides. Closed forms come from Faulhaber's formulas, geometric sums and telescoping.

How the answer is checked

Closed forms are compared with direct summation, and limits of sums are checked numerically.

Typing your input

  • Finite sums are exact when possible; with an end index like n you get a closed form (Faulhaber, geometric, telescoping). Infinite series are tested for convergence (nth-term, ratio, p-series, alternating) and summed accurately.
  • Use k as the index, for example 1/k^2.
  • Leave the upper limit as inf for an infinite series.

Questions

Can it find the radius of convergence?

Yes, choose the radius task: you get the ratio-test radius and a verdict at each endpoint.
