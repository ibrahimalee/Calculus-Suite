Unit converter

Convert between units by typing naturally, such as 5 km/h to m/s or 72 degF to degC. Incompatible units are refused with an explanation.

How it works

Units are parsed with their dimensions, so compound units such as km/h or kg·m/s² convert correctly.

How the answer is checked

The dimensions of both sides are compared before converting.

Typing your input

  • Length, mass, time, temperature (degC, degF, K), speed, area, volume, energy, power, pressure … with + − × ÷ and “to”.
  • Write value unit to unit, for example 1 mile to km.

Questions

Why does it refuse my conversion?

Because the dimensions differ, for example metres to seconds.
