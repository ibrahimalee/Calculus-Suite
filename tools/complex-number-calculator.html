How it works

Residues are computed at every pole of a rational function, and contour integrals follow from the residue theorem. Domain colouring shows zeros and poles of f(z) at a glance.

How the answer is checked

Contour and real integrals are compared with direct numerical integration.

Typing your input

  • Use i for the imaginary unit and z as the variable.

Questions

Can it evaluate real integrals with residues?

Yes, integrals over the whole real line of rational functions, checked numerically.
