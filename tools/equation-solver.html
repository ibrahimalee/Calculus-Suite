How it works

Polynomials are factored and solved exactly, linear and quadratic equations come with a worked solution, and other equations are solved numerically on the interval you choose. Inequalities are solved by finding the critical points and testing each interval.

How the answer is checked

Every root is substituted back into the original equation, and each interval of an inequality is tested at an interior point.

Typing your input

  • Polynomials give every root (real and complex); other equations are solved numerically. Use <, >, ≤, ≥ for inequalities.
  • Use < <= > >= for inequalities.
  • Set the search range for equations such as cos(x) = x.

Questions

Does it find complex roots?

Yes, for polynomials, listed with their multiplicity.
