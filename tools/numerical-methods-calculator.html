How it works

Interpolating polynomials are built with divided differences in exact fractions. Splines can be natural, clamped or not-a-knot. Quadrature rules are compared side by side on the same integral.

How the answer is checked

Splines are checked for C² continuity at every knot; quadrature results against a high-accuracy value.

Typing your input

  • Give points as x, y pairs separated by ;

Questions

What is the Runge phenomenon?

High-degree interpolation at equally spaced points can oscillate wildly near the ends; Chebyshev nodes avoid it. The example shows both.
